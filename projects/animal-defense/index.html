Animal Defense

Educational tower defense game about the food chain

  • RoleGame Designer · Level Designer · Lead Programmer
  • EngineUnity
  • PlatformPC (800×600)

Highlights

  • Teaches the food chain through combat rules: each level of the chain is strong against the one it feeds on
  • Humans hit everyone, and decomposers boost the gold earned from anything that dies in their area

Leadership

  • Lead programmer, game designer and level designer on a project planned and built in under 6 months

Overview

A course project tied to the Games and Society class. Planned and built in under six months, the game teaches how the food chain works. I was the lead programmer, game designer and level designer. It was designed for 800×600 screens.

Rules

  • Producers slow enemies; primary consumers deal 50% damage to producers
  • Secondary consumers deal 50% damage to primary consumers
  • Tertiary consumers deal 50% damage to secondary consumers
  • Humans deal 25% damage to everyone, except tertiary consumers, where they deal 50%
  • Decomposers increase the gold generated by anything that dies inside their area
Technical deep dive

Waypoint system

The game’s waypoint system still works, and its concept is what I reach for when building a new one. I prefer to rewrite small systems like this instead of reusing them, so I can compare solutions and see how much I’ve changed, both as a programmer and in how I think.
