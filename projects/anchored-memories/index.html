Overview

Anchored Memories is a narrative-driven virtual reality experience about climate-induced migration in Central Texas. Guided by a character through a “memoryscape”, the player moves through five acts while spatial audio and a full-body haptic vest carry memory, loss and displacement. A research layer pairs the experience with biometric feedback.

It was showcased in July 2026 at the Texas Immersive Institute (Moody College of Communication, UT Austin) and continues as a prototype for festivals and museums.

Technical deep dive

Why Unity

The team originally planned to build in Unreal Engine. When I joined as Unity lead for D2R Studios, we moved the project to Unity, the engine I know best, so that a small, part-time team could still reach a working deliverable in time for the showcase. I also brought in assets from my personal library to cut down on production time.
