Overview

Made in a few days around Christmas 2014 with a friend, as practice and as a gift: a tiny virtual pet based on a real person, to carry around on a phone. Neither of us knew anything about mobile development yet, and Unity didn’t have its Canvas UI system.

Its one quirk: she loves chocolate milk, but it has to be Nescau. Food is random, and if she gets a Toddy instead, she gets mad. The screenshots were taken in the BlueStacks emulator back then. For a few days of work with no mobile experience, it turned out pretty good.
