Kiosk app · Client: Fiat

Fiat Personalização

Touchscreen app to design custom vehicle stickers and preview them in 3D

  • RoleDeveloper (intern)
  • EngineUnity
  • PlatformTouchscreen PC, promotional event
  • ForFiat · D2R Studios

Highlights

  • Guided kiosk flow for event visitors: enter your details, pick a car and a premade or custom design, then place the sticker on the car

Overview

Customers created their own sticker by choosing colors and shapes, previewed it applied to the vehicle in a 3D simulation, and could then buy it on an e-commerce platform. It ran on a touchscreen PC at a Fiat promotional stand at Minas Tênis Clube, Belo Horizonte.

Technical deep dive

How it works

  1. Enter your name by tapping “Nome” and typing it in.
  2. Enter your email.
  3. Enter your birth year. It stays hidden on screen for privacy.
  4. Choose the car settings, then pick a premade design or create your own by choosing shapes, details and icons. You can rotate the camera at any time.

Challenges

We had 10 days to build it, mostly as a pitch from D2R to Fiat. I was an intern with a year and a half of Unity experience, and the hardest part was the decal system: deforming a plane to fit the car’s mesh. I researched until I found a plugin approach that worked within the deadline.

The code belongs to the client, so I can’t show it here.
